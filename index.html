How to play 📖

• You start with 3 cards, enemy with 4. Each turn: +1 mana crystal (up to 10), refill mana, draw a card.
• Mana: card cost is the blue gem. You can't overspend.
• Summoning sickness: minions can't attack the turn played unless they have ⚡ Charge / 💨 Rush (Rush can't hit heroes turn 1).
• Keywords: 🛡️ Taunt must be attacked first • 🛡️✨ Divine Shield blocks first hit • 💨 Windfury attacks twice • ☠️ Deathrattle triggers on death • 📯 Battlecry triggers on play.
• Fatigue: empty deck = 1,2,3… damage when drawing.
• Win: reduce Malakor to 0 ❤️. Lose: he does it to you first. Good luck!
