❓ How to Play

  • 🏃 WASD / arrows to run, Space to jump (double-jump allowed!).
  • 🖐 Near a soft glowing clay blob, press E to reshape it: Block → Ramp → Bridge → Block…
  • 🔮 Collect beads. 🌸 Secret flowers hide off the main path.
  • 🚩 Touch checkpoint flags to save your spot. 🏁 Reach the golden arch to finish.
  • 🔺 Spikes send you back to the last flag.
  • 📱 On touch screens use the left stick + ▲ button.
  • 🧱 The Level Editor lets you build + playtest workshop levels (saved in your browser).
