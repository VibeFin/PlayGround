MINECRAFT 3D

Pocket Voxel Edition — made for Phone & Tablet

🌍 Infinite-feel terrain + trees + water
🕹️ Twin-stick touch: move + look
⛏️ Break & place • Fly mode • Save

Phone: left stick = walk · drag right = look · JUMP / BREAK / PLACE buttons
Desktop: WASD + mouse (click to lock) · Space jump · LMB break · RMB place · 1-8 blocks · F fly

How to play

  • 🕹️ Left thumb stick — walk / fly
  • 👉 Drag anywhere on right half — look around
  • ⬆ JUMP — jump (double-tap & hold in Fly to go up)
  • ⛏ BREAK — remove aimed block (crosshair)
  • 🧱 PLACE — place selected hotbar block
  • 🕊️ Fly — toggle flying for building
  • Progress auto-saves on this device.
