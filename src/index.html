📱 How to play on mobile

  • Left thumb-stick to move / strafe
  • Drag right side to turn & aim

  • Add to Home Screen for fullscreen

🖥️ Desktop controls

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump
  • Click canvas for pointer-lock, mouse to look
  • Click / F to fire · R respawn bots

⚖️ Real Quake 3 on phones?

Want the original? You need your own baseq3/pak0.pk3 + an open engine:

  • Android: OpenArena2026 / ioquake3 ports (Deltatouch, Kwaak3, sdlioq3a) on Play Store / F-Droid
  • iOS / Apple TV: Quake3-iOS (tomkidd) — build with Xcode + copy baseq3
  • Browser: dos.zone/q3 works on Android + iOS
